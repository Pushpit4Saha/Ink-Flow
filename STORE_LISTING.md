**Long description:**
[APP_NAME] turns everything you read into notes you can find again.

🌸 Select & note — select any word or sentence on any website, click the flower button, and write what it means, your thoughts, or add a photo.
🖍️ Highlights come back — when you open the page again, your notes are highlighted right where you left them.
📄 PDFs too — open online PDFs or files from your computer in the built-in PDF viewer and annotate them. Your notes return every time you open the same PDF.
✨ AI meanings — get a short meaning of the selected text (English, Bangla and more). Press Tab to use it, or just write your own.
🏷️ Folders & tags — organise notes, search everything, filter by website or date.
📤 Export & share — export to Word or PDF, or share a note, folder or tag with a link.
📱 Everywhere — all notes sync to your dashboard, which you can install as an app on your phone.

Your notes are private unless you choose to share them.

**Single purpose (Chrome asks for this):**
Let users attach sticky notes to text they select on web pages and PDFs, and see those notes again later.

**Permission justifications (Chrome asks for each):**
- *Host permission (all sites):* needed to show the note button and the user's highlights on any page they read, and to open online PDFs in our viewer.
- *storage / unlimitedStorage:* keeps the login session, settings, and notes saved while offline until they can sync.
- *activeTab:* the keyboard shortcut adds a note to the selection on the current tab.
- *alarms:* retries uploading notes saved while offline.
- *Remote code:* No, all code is in the package.

**Data use disclosures:** collects "Website content" (only the text the user selects and the page address, when they save a note) and "Authentication information" (login session). Not sold, not used for ads, not used for credit/lending.
